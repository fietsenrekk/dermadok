Varicose veins and vascular care

Duplex assessment, conservative management, sclerocompression and the Müller procedure — assessed by vascular surgeon and dermatologist together.

If you have varicose veins you can come to us for an assessment, which includes a duplex scan. A duplex scan combines ultrasound of the blood vessels with measurement of the blood flow speed within them. The vessels are imaged, and colour is used to show flow direction and speed.

Dr. Wustenberghs also treats phlebitis and thrombosis, lymphoedema, arterial disease and claudication, aneurysm, and chronic wounds.

Treatment options

Conservative

We look at which compression stockings or other measures can best limit your symptoms.

Sclerocompression

Veins are injected with a fluid. Larger veins are treated with foam (foam sclerotherapy), with or without ultrasound guidance.

Müller procedure

The veins are removed under local anaesthetic through very small incisions. Particularly suited to large varicose veins. Any small remaining veins can be injected later.

Referral

If the veins are too large for the above, you are referred to a hospital for a larger operation or endovenous treatment.
