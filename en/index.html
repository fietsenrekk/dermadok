Three lines of care

01 — DERMATOLOGY

Conditions of the skin, hair and nails — from eczema and psoriasis to mole monitoring and the treatment of skin cancer.

  • UV treatment
  • Monitoring of moles and skin cancer, with specialised digital photography
  • Patch testing to identify contact allergy
  • Removal of moles and skin cancer under local anaesthetic

Read more →

02 — SURGERY

Day procedures under local anaesthetic. You do not stay at the clinic and go home immediately afterwards.

  • Removal of lipomas
  • Removal of cysts
  • Scar correction
  • Removal of skin lesions or moles
  • Lymph node biopsies

Read more →

03 — AESTHETIC MEDICINE

Facial treatments with Dr. Van den Steen, with natural results as the starting point.

  • Muscle relaxants
  • Fillers — hyaluronic acid in various viscosities
  • Skinboosters
  • PRF and PRP, using your own blood
  • Dr.CYJ Hairfiller
